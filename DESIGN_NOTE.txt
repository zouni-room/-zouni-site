瑪瑙 / AGATE — Banded Agate × After Hours edition

Design idea:
- Actual agate is represented by a visible banded-stone SVG illustration, plus layered mineral-band patterns on the banners and backgrounds.
- Palette combines milky quartz-like cream, gray-lilac, muted moss/sage, and deep after-hours indigo/charcoal.
- The site draws on the mood of Ayase's 「夜撫でるメノウ」 without using lyrics or official cover art.
- The homepage includes an embedded YouTube player for Ayase's official music video (not autoplaying), plus a direct YouTube link.
- The top page has only three main banners: guide, creative, and supported works. Name/marker information remains within the guide page, not a separate banner.

Files:
- index.html: home page and embedded music player
- guide.html: stance, interaction, waiting-room notes, name/marker explanation
- creative.html: creative work information
- works.html: supported works and character search
- style.css: responsive design, agate palette and theme switch
- script.js: mobile menu, theme switch, character search, back-to-top
- agate-stone.svg: original vector illustration of layered agate bands
